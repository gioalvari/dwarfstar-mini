# F8: qwen3 prefill and decode against llama.cpp

Goal: close the gap with llama.cpp on the qwen3 models (1.7B dense, 30B-A3B
MoE) without changing the qwen35 path.

Starting point, measured in one session (tok/s, DSM vs `llama-bench -fa 1`):

| Model | pp570 / pp512 | pp3990 / pp4096 | decode / tg128 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.7B Q4_K_M | 1135 / 1825 | 443 / 1481 | 127.6 / 127.1 |
| 1.7B Q5_K_M | 1056 / 1566 | 436 / 1383 | 58.9 / 115.9 |
| 1.7B Q8_0 | 1171 / 1823 | 455 / 1546 | 92.0 / 87.8 |
| 30B-A3B asym | 581 / 773 | 300 / 647 | 62.1 / 61.9 |

The matmuls were already at llama.cpp speed (5.2-5.5 TFLOP/s). Prefill was
held back by attention: `attention_prefill_d128_tiled` took 35% of the 1.7B
prefill at 570 tokens and 76% at 3990, at ~0.26 TFLOP/s. Decode was at
parity except on Q5_K, where `q5k_gemv` reached 49-68 GB/s.

## Milestones

| Milestone | Commit | Change | Kill switch | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | `32670f3` | Flash attention for qwen3 prefill, ported from llama.cpp `kernel_flash_attn_ext_f32_dk128_dv128`. Uses simdgroup-matrix QK and PV and reads K from device memory (11 KiB threadgroup memory). Unwritten KV rows are masked so no NaN can leak in. | `DSM_ATTN_FA=0` | 1.7B 570: ~1000 → ~1390; 3990: ~465 → ~1113 (2.4x). 30B 570: ~565 → ~600; 3990: ~245 → ~360 (1.45x) |
| M2 | `7d69d9a` | Q5_K GEMV ported from llama.cpp `kernel_mul_mv_q5_K_f32` | - | 1.7B Q5_K_M decode ~59 → ~100-114 |
| M3 | `bc1ea66` | Fused decode dispatches: `qk_norm_rope` writes K straight into the cache, and `add_rmsnorm` merges the residual add with the next norm | `DSM_DECODE_FUSE=0` | Greedy output byte-identical; decode +2-5% (Q17 +1.6%, Q8 +3.8%, Q15 +5.3%, Q30 +3.6%, Qwen3.6 +1.8%) |
| M4 | `12a3766` | `bench-gemv` micro-benchmark only | - | The output-head kernels already use llama.cpp's dispatch shapes. The head runs at ~209 GB/s against 357 GB/s on mid-size Q8_0 tensors, so the dispatch shape is not the limit |

Not done:
- M5: 16-row `mul_mm_id` tiles for experts that get few tokens on the 30B.
  Estimated +5-8% prefill.
- M6: the flash-attention kernel at head dim 256 for qwen35. It would replace
  `attention_prefill_d256` (19 KiB of threadgroup memory).

## Correctness

- `check-attn` (`tools/attn_check.m`) compares fa128 with
  `attention_prefill_d128_tiled` over 42 cases: rows 9-570, pos0 0/100,
  GQA 16/8 and 32/4. Max relative error is 7.4e-7, with no non-finite values,
  including when the unwritten KV rows are filled with NaN.
- `check-prefill` references on the 570-token prompt after F8
  (max_abs / KL / max_dprob):
  - Q17: 0.0103 / 1.4e-9 / 9.5e-7
  - Q30: 0.528 / 3.8e-7 / 3.0e-6
  - Q15: 0.0110 / 4.1e-10 / 4.2e-7
  - Q8: 0.0123 / 2.7e-9 / 1.2e-6
  - Qwen3.6 and the 27B: dumps byte-identical to before F8.
- Edge lengths n 63/64/65/1025, split 300 and chunk 9 pass on the 1.7B. On the
  30B, n 63/64/65 fail the top-5 check both with FA on and with FA off: this is
  a near-tie on the short repeated prompt and predates F8.
- `DSM_ATTN_FA=0` and `DSM_DECODE_FUSE=0` give logits byte-identical to the
  parent commits. `check-rewind` gives KL 0.
- Regression found after F8: M3 also skipped the attention norm in dense
  qwen35 layers after layer 0, but the dense FFN tail ends with a plain add,
  not `add_rmsnorm`, so the 27B decoded garbage. The F8 gates did not cover the
  27B decode path. Fixed in `215006a` (the hybrid layer always runs its norm);
  the 27B is back to its reference logits and to llama.cpp greedy output.

## Result (2026-11-12, same session, warm)

| Model | DSM pp570 | llama pp512 | DSM pp3990 | llama pp4096 | DSM decode | llama tg128 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1.7B Q4_K_M | 1670 | 2048 | 1222 | 1735 | 136 | 149.5 |
| 1.7B Q5_K_M | 1568 | 1909 | 1176 | 1641 | 113.8 | 125.2 |
| 1.7B Q8_0 | 1784 | 2126 | 1275 | 1803 | 95.7 | 103.0 |
| 30B-A3B asym | 680 | 828 | 355 | ~473 | 64.7 | 63.2 |
| Qwen3.6-35B-A3B UD-Q4_K_S | 674-693 | 719 | 522 | 351 | 55.4 | 55.6 |
| Qwen3.6-35B-A3B UD-Q4_K_M | 614 | 778 | 541 | 367 | 49.9 | 54.3 |

Compared with llama.cpp:
- The 1.7B is at 82-84% in short prefill (it was 60-64%), 70-72% at 4k (was
  ~30%), and 91-93% in decode.
- The 30B is at 82% in prefill, ~75% at 4k and at parity in decode.
- At 4k, the hybrid Qwen3.6 models prefill about 1.5x faster than llama.cpp.

Run-to-run variance is about ±5-10%; the same configuration ranged from 624 to
693 tok/s across runs on Qwen3.6 pp570.
